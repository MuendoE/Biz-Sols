The Challenge

Running Trials Well is Hard on a Site

Modern trials demand current regulatory submissions, GCP-trained staff, disciplined SOPs, continuous inspection-readiness and dedicated technology, all while the clinic keeps caring for patients.

New sites: eager, but unsure how to start

Keen to run a first study, but how do you become compliant and credible to sponsors? The regulatory pathway, the SOPs, the training and the systems all have to exist before the first patient is screened.

Established sites: capable, but at capacity

Stretched staff, ageing SOPs, manual data handling and recurring audit findings cap how much you can take on and hold you back from more complex, higher-value studies.

The Differentiator

Software Few Consultancies Can Offer a Site

We build our own clinical research software, so partner sites run on a modern, compliant stack without licence barriers or a build project of their own.

Clinveo EDC / CDMS: Flagship

Regulated electronic data capture: study & eCRF design, edit checks, e-signatures and an append-only audit trail, with 21 CFR Part 11 controls. Live demo available on request.

See the EDC

IWRS / RTSM

Subject randomisation and drug-supply management, integrated with the EDC over a signed API so results write straight back to the eCRF.

Clinical Trial Patient Matcher

Matches protocol eligibility criteria to consented EHR records and returns a ranked list of eligible patients, with a full audit trail.

LMS & Dashboards

A hosted learning platform for GCP and protocol training, plus live recruitment and data dashboards with sponsor-ready reporting views.
