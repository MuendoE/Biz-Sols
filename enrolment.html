Enrolment in Four Steps

Getting started is simple. We'll enrol you as soon as your payment is confirmed.

1

Create your account

Register free on our learning portal at learn.alphacrsolutions.co.za and confirm your email.

2

Request enrolment

Email enrolments@alphacrsolutions.co.za with the course you'd like to join. Fees are on our GCP training page, and we quote CRA simulations and groups of 10 or more.

3

Make payment

Pay the course fee by EFT using the banking details below, or securely online by card via PayFast. Then send your proof of payment, with your name and the course, to payments@alphacrsolutions.co.za.

4

Get enrolled & start

Once we receive your proof of payment, we enrol you in the course and you will receive an email confirmation. Log in and start learning right away.

Enrolment FAQs

Common questions about enrolling in our GCP training and CRA simulations in South Africa.

How do I enrol in an Alpha CR Solutions course?

Create a free account on our learning portal, email enrolments@alphacrsolutions.co.za to request your course, pay the fee by EFT or online card, and we enrol you as soon as your payment reflects.

How much do the GCP training and CRA simulation courses cost?

Good Clinical Practice Foundation Course: R3,500 per person.
Groups of 10 or more: 10% discount, R3,150 per person.
ICH E6(R3) GCP course: R575 per person.
CRA simulation fees vary by programme. Email enrolments@alphacrsolutions.co.za with the simulation you are interested in and we will send current pricing and payment details.

What payment methods do you accept?

You can pay by EFT / bank transfer or by debit or credit card online via PayFast, South Africa’s trusted payment gateway.

Can I cancel and get a refund?

Yes. If you cancel before enrolment takes effect you receive an 80% refund (we retain a 20% administrative fee). Once you have been given access to the course, fees are non-refundable. Email refunds@alphacrsolutions.co.za to cancel. Full details are in our Refund & Cancellation Policy.

Do I receive a certificate?

Yes. On completing the course and its assessment you receive an Alpha CR Solutions certificate of completion, evidencing the training you have undertaken.

What happens if I fail three attempts?

Please contact Alpha CR Solutions. We will discuss options on an individual basis.

Is the GCP course HPCSA accredited?

Yes. Our Good Clinical Practice Foundation Course is accredited by HPCSA and you will earn CPD points for the activity. HPCSA-registered participants earn 24 CEUs: 16 General and 8 Ethics, Human Rights and Medical Law, on passing the final assessment at 80%. Delegates who are not registered with the HPCSA, such as pharmacists and nurses, receive an attendance certificate only. See the course details.

Is the GCP training aligned with ICH GCP?

Yes, our Good Clinical Practice training is aligned with ICH E6(R3) GCP and contextualised for South African investigative sites and SAHPRA requirements.

Who are the courses for?

Our training suits Clinical Research Associates (CRAs), investigators, study coordinators and site staff, from new entrants to experienced clinical research professionals looking to build or prove their competency.
