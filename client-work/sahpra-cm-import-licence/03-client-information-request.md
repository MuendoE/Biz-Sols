## D. Products ★

**Complete the attached `02-product-schedule.csv` in full.** For each line we specifically
need the following, and the application cannot proceed without them:

| # | Item | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| D1 | Exact proprietary name as it will appear on the South African label | Must match the licence, the product and the import documents |
| D2 | Dosage form and pack size | Licence schedule field |
| D3 | **Full quantitative composition per dosage unit** — actives and excipients | Classification and scheduling |
| D4 | **Recommended daily dose, and the quantity of each active per recommended daily dose** | South African scheduling thresholds are expressed per recommended daily dose |
| D5 | For Selenium: the selenium salt used and the micrograms of elemental selenium per tablet and per recommended daily dose | **Determines whether the product is a health supplement at all.** Highest priority item in this request |
| D6 | For Marine Collagen: species, source country, and whether the material is fish or bovine derived | Animal-origin import control may require a separate veterinary permit |
| D7 | For Black Garlic: the form (whole, extract, extract ratio, solvent) and whether it is sold as a food or a supplement elsewhere | Determines foodstuff versus medicine |
| D8 | For Psyllium Husk: the exact claims made on the label and in marketing | A therapeutic claim makes it a medicine |
| D9 | For Vegan Omega 3: the algal source, and EPA and DHA content per dosage unit | Permitted levels and claims |
| D10 | Shelf life and storage conditions | Licence schedule and SOP for shelf-life determination |
| D11 | Finished product specification and a representative certificate of analysis | Attestation evidence |

**Documents per product:** current label artwork, front and back, legible · carton and leaflet
· finished product specification · certificate of analysis · composition breakdown signed by
the manufacturer · stability data or shelf-life justification · certificate of free sale from
the country of origin · evidence of marketing status elsewhere.
