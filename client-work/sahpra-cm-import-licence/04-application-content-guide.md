# Application Content Guide
## Assembling and submitting the Category D import licence application

**Status:** draft. The portal navigation, licence type codes and form fields below are taken
from secondary summaries of SAHPRA guidance and **must be checked against the live portal and
the current guideline** before the application is built. See `08-source-register.md`.

---

## 1. Route

Applications for Category D (complementary medicines) licences are made through SAHPRA's
dedicated complementary medicines platform, reported as **www.sahpracm.org.za**. The
applicant registers as a site user, then navigates:

> Applications → Licensing → APPLY → 1. New Applications

and selects the licence type. `[VERIFY navigation and URL]`

The general pharmaceutical licensing mailbox (**pharmalicensing@sahpra.org.za**) handles
manufacturer, importer, exporter and wholesaler applications outside the Category D platform
and is the right channel for the scoping question in §2. `[VERIFY address]`

## 2. Settle the licence type before building anything

| Type | Scope | Our position |
|---|---|---|
| DL01 | Manufacture, import or export Category D medicines | Not applicable — client does not manufacture |
| **DL02** | Import or export Category D medicines | **Required** — the import activity |
| **DL03** | Wholesale or distribute Category D medicines | **Required** — the client sells to retailers (Decision D1, settled) |

**Before paying any fee**, put these three questions to SAHPRA Pharma Licensing in writing
and keep the reply on file:

1. For an importer that does not manufacture and holds no registered products, is DL02 the
   correct type? (One published description attaches DL02 to "holders of certificate of
   registration", which cannot be read literally while no complementary medicine is
   registered.)
2. The client imports and sells on to retailers, so both DL02 and DL03 are required. Is
   that **one combined application or two separate applications**, are two fees payable,
   and are both activities reflected on a single licence or on two?
3. If any product proves to contain a Schedule 1 or higher substance, does it fall out of the
   Category D route entirely and into SAHPGL-LIC-01/-02?

## 3. Application content

Information required of an applicant is prescribed by regulation 23 of the General
Regulations, read with the Category D roadmap guideline. Expect to supply:

### 3.1 Applicant
- Legal name, CIPC registration number, trading name, registered and physical addresses
- Ownership and directorship
- Contact person and correspondence details
- Any existing SAHPRA licence or application references

### 3.2 Activity and scope
- The activity applied for: **import** and **wholesale/distribute** (both — see §2)
- Every premises at which either activity is conducted
- The product schedule (see `02-product-schedule.md`)
- The classes of customer to be supplied, and the basis on which the client satisfies itself
  that each is entitled to receive the product

### 3.3 Responsible person
- Identity, qualifications, SAPC registration where applicable
- CV
- Letter of appointment by the licence holder **and** letter of acceptance by the appointee
- Defined authority over release, quarantine, recall and communication with SAHPRA

### 3.4 Premises
- Physical address and site/floor plan showing receiving, quarantine, released stock,
  rejected/recalled stock and dispatch
- Storage conditions, temperature monitoring and calibration
- Security and access control
- Pest control

### 3.5 Quality system and attestation evidence
Either a valid GMP certificate for each manufacturing site, or the full information set
required by the application including Annexure C of SAHPGL-PEM-COMP-02. `[VERIFY Annexure C]`

Minimum SOPs: quality assurance product release; recall; finished product specifications and
testing; determination of shelf life. Where the activity has not commenced, SOPs plus blank
record templates are acceptable; once commenced, completed records for at least the preceding
twelve months must be held. `[VERIFY]`

### 3.6 Fees
Proof of payment referenced per SAHPRA's fee categorisation guidance. See
`07-fees-timelines-submission.md`.

## 5. Pre-submission quality check

Run this before anything is submitted. Each line is a known cause of a query or a rejection.

- [ ] Licence type confirmed in writing with SAHPRA, including whether DL02 + DL03 is one application or two (§2)
- [ ] Both activities — import **and** wholesale/distribution — stated on the application and in the covering letter
- [ ] Applicant legal name identical across CIPC certificate, application, covering letter and proof of payment
- [ ] Every product's proprietary name identical across schedule, label artwork and supplier documentation
- [ ] Composition stated per dosage unit **and** per recommended daily dose
- [ ] **Selenium scheduling position resolved and documented** (memo §5.1)
- [ ] Classification decision recorded for Black Garlic and Psyllium Husk, with reasons
- [ ] Product range re-confirmed with the client as closed at five lines, with the amendment cost of any later addition explained (Decision D2, settled)
- [ ] Manufacturing site addresses are the actual sites, not brand owners or traders
- [ ] Every GMP certificate valid at the date of submission, with expiry dates diarised
- [ ] Responsible person appointment and acceptance letters both signed and dated
- [ ] Site plan marks quarantine and rejected-stock areas
- [ ] SOP set complete, version controlled, signed, dated and effective
- [ ] Labels checked against regulations 10, 11 and 12, including the unregistered-medicine disclaimer
- [ ] Animal-origin import requirements checked for Marine Collagen
- [ ] Customer qualification procedure in place, and consistent with the schedule status of every line (a Schedule 1 product may not be supplied to general retail)
- [ ] Distribution records demonstrate batch-level traceability to each customer
- [ ] Trading terms, promotional plans and pricing tested against ss 18A, 18B and 22G
- [ ] Fee paid, correct amount, correct reference, proof attached
- [ ] Annexures numbered per §4 and cross-referenced from the covering letter
- [ ] Complete copy of the submission retained in the client file, with the submission receipt

## 6. After submission

- Log the submission reference and date on the day of submission.
- Expect screening and queries. Diarise a follow-up if nothing is received — do not let a
  query deadline pass unanswered, as applications are closed for non-response.
- Answer queries in a single consolidated response per round, cross-referenced to the
  original annexure numbering.
- On issue, check the licence face for: correct legal entity, correct activity, correct
  premises, correct product schedule, validity dates. Errors are easier to correct
  immediately than at renewal.
