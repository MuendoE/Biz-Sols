# Source Register and Verification Log

**Read this before relying on anything in this pack.**

The pack was drafted in an environment whose network policy blocked outbound access to
`sahpra.org.za`, to `saflii.org` and to the other sites hosting the Medicines Act schedules
and the Category D guideline. Content was therefore established from **search-engine
summaries** of those documents rather than from the documents themselves.

That is sufficient to build the right structure and ask the right questions. It is **not**
sufficient to advise a client or to submit an application. Every item below must be confirmed
against a downloaded copy of the current document before the pack leaves Alpha CR Solutions.

---

## 1. Verification checklist — in priority order
